rule: a numbered attribute "NN_x of M" has its title as name "M'NN_x" (--fix-name writes it),
  only when x and M are names (no space, "(", ")", "'", "!", ...).

=== A. "NN_output_(logoview) of X": a "_(...)" part glued to the attribute word (13)
  why: "output_(logoview)" with "_(" is NOT a name; the form "05_output (logoview) of X" (space before "(") is:
    the "(logoview)" then is a new name and the attribute is "output of X" (like "04_input (inptview) of X").
  fix: "05_output_(logoview)" → "05_output (logoview)"; then --fix-name writes X'05_output.
